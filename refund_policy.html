Refund Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

1. General Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your FlixNChill Premium subscription. If you are not satisfied with our service, we offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all annual subscriptions.

2. Eligibility

To be eligible for a refund, you must request it within 14 days of your initial purchase. Refunds are generally not provided for mid-year cancellations after the initial 14-day period.

3. Processing

Our payments are processed by our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy. When a refund is approved, it will be processed back to your original payment method. Please allow 5-10 business days for the funds to appear in your account.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact our support team at flixnchill@soltrixstudios.com with your account email address and order number.
